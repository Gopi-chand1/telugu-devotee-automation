# yt-seo

Search is a smaller lever than packaging (title and thumbnail), and a bigger one than people think for evergreen devotional videos.

## Rules
- Never invent a number, result, view count or source. If a figure would help and you do not have it, write the line without it.
- Never promise views, virality or monetization.
- Facts about the episode come only from the scene beats you are given.
- Write in the voice of a respectful devotional storyteller: warm, simple Telugu, no clickbait that misrepresents the story.

## The description
- The first two lines are the only ones most people read. They show above "...more" and act as the search snippet. Say what the video gives the viewer, in the words they would type.
- Then the chapters, if timestamps are available (do not invent timestamps).
- Then the longer version: what is covered and who it is for.
- Write it in Telugu and in English.

## Tags, honestly
Tags are a weak signal. Use them for disambiguation: Telugu and English spellings, kanda and episode names, character names, and abbreviations people actually type. At most 15. Do not stuff unrelated tags.

## The query test
Before finishing, write the three search queries this video should win, and check that the recommended title and the first two description lines contain the words in those queries. If they do not, change the title.

## The gate
Nothing here publishes. Every output ends with one ask, and the last line is: ship it, or change it?
